## Notes after Yan's replies (2026-11-20)
- **Mind Weaver adds (undecided).** Options: (a) no adds; (b) **puppets**: Husks strung on its threads, each thread can be cut to free (stun) the Husk, so the adds are part of the thread mechanic and need no new art (my pick); (c) the small hand-built Mind Weavers. The small Mind Weaver stays a normal Tier 3 mutant either way.
- **The Excavator**: liked. Built like the eel: authored moves (sweep, dig-slam, scoop-and-throw), telegraphed hazard zones, a targetable hydraulics weak point; "board its arm" would be a later extra.
- **The Marshal and the portal computer** (how it would work): the fight is an ordinary Visor/Spool-style duel in three phases. The portal computer is just an interactable object (same system as the lab consoles/switches) behind the arena, and the portal a zone exit. When the Marshal breaks, a 90 s countdown starts and a choice appears: shoot/smash the computer (portal collapses, flag set) or push through before it closes (exit opens). All pieces exist (interactables, exits, timers like the Barrier's); it is content work, not new tech.
- **Tier 2 had no mutant boss**: added the Singer (3a). With it, the Pitman (3b) can drop to a plain story encounter.

## Order I would build in (cheapest, most reuse first)
1. Pitman (T2) and the Hook (T1): reuse the Eel/Visor frames, human or Husk art only.
2. Mother Hollow (T1): scaled Prowler art + the Elder's wave code.
3. Array Commander (T5) and the Marshal (T6): human duels with timers, no new creature art.
4. The Mind Weaver (T5): the arena is built, the art exists.
5. The Ferryman, the Specimen rematch, the Excavator, the Choir, the Orderly: new art each, so spread them out as art credits allow.
6. The Calm Voice and the Mind last, with the ending.
