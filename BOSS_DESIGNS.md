# Boss creature designs (2026-11-20; Yan: "silent hill, resident evil, bloodborne style monsters")
Direction words (for the artist, never shown to players): wrongness over gore, one strong silhouette each, a human thing inside the monster (hands, a mouth, a uniform), a rhythm or a sound as the signature (the 11 s beat), and a single unreadable part (a face with no eyes, a mask). Muted palette plus ONE accent colour per boss. Original names only. Concept sprites are hand-built (`ArtSource/make_boss_concepts.py`), NOT in the game, and are concept quality, not final art.

The rest are briefs only. Mother Hollow needed a second pass (first: a flat bean body, bright purple button sacs, a small head); the Ferryman worked first time (the pale face pressed against the lantern glass).

Specimen grown and Excavator each needed two passes (first: a flat pink disc with cartoon eyes, a clean toy digger). Honest state: concept quality; the Excavator's vent growths read a bit like mushrooms and the husk in its cab is hard to read at small size.

**Husks in concepts use the real Husk sprites** (`Resources/Sprites/husk_atlas_x2.png`, front cells pasted 1:1 by `make_boss_concepts.husk_cell`): the Excavator's driver is cell 40, the Choir's column cells 3-47. Yan, 2026-11-20: the Husk already exists, so a reference must show it, otherwise the sprite maker invents a different one. The Choir (179x282): a stone bell tower with a lit brass bell and sound rings, and a dense column of Husks climbing it, every mouth open, joined mouth to mouth by red sinew.

**The Choir is not an art job** (Yan, 2026-11-20): it is a bell tower built on the Abandoned City map plus waves of the existing Husk units, so the 18-Husk column concept above is only a mood reference. Needs: tower tiles/props, the bell as a targetable object, wave spawns (like the Barrier/Crown waves), no new sprites.

## The Verger (concept, 2026-11-20; Yan: "give me the concept for that boss we talked about")
Gothic beast hunter, the cathedral's last verger (human boss on the Visor/Spool frame, `MISSION_CHOIR.md`). `ArtSource/make_verger_concept.py` -> `mutants_anim/boss_concepts/verger_concept_sheet.png` (+ `verger_guard_concept.png`, `verger_chop_concept.png`), hand-built with pxart, concept quality, NOT in the game. **Look:** a tall figure in a long dark coat with a high collar, ragged tails and a belt of pouches and blood vials; a wide-brimmed hat that drops his face into shadow, pale eyes, a stitched leather mask over the nose and mouth with a brass clasp; a red scarf (the one colour accent) hanging from the neck; gloves; boots. **Weapon:** a huge two-handed bearded greataxe (wide serrated cheek, a long hooked beard for the drag-pull, a back spike, a wrapped grip with a brass ring), blood on the steel. **Poses drawn:** guard (the axe held low across the body) and the overhead wind-up. **To make it a game sprite:** his coat, hat and mask as armour looks on the existing NPC body (`make_npc_looks.py`, like the Barbwire Coat), a held-axe weapon sprite, and moves with `melee_moves.py`.
