# Boss creature designs (2026-11-20; Yan: "silent hill, resident evil, bloodborne style monsters")
Direction words (for the artist, never shown to players): wrongness over gore, one strong silhouette each, a human thing inside the monster (hands, a mouth, a uniform), a rhythm or a sound as the signature (the 11 s beat), and a single unreadable part (a face with no eyes, a mask). Muted palette plus ONE accent colour per boss. Original names only. Concept sprites are hand-built (`ArtSource/make_boss_concepts.py`), NOT in the game, and are concept quality, not final art.

## The Verger (concept, 2026-11-20; Yan: "give me the concept for that boss we talked about")
Gothic beast hunter, the cathedral's last verger (human boss on the Visor/Spool frame, `MISSION_CHOIR.md`). `ArtSource/make_verger_concept.py` -> `mutants_anim/boss_concepts/verger_concept_sheet.png` (+ `verger_guard_concept.png`, `verger_chop_concept.png`), hand-built with pxart, concept quality, NOT in the game. **Look:** a tall figure in a long dark coat with a high collar, ragged tails and a belt of pouches and blood vials; a wide-brimmed hat that drops his face into shadow, pale eyes, a stitched leather mask over the nose and mouth with a brass clasp; a red scarf (the one colour accent) hanging from the neck; gloves; boots. **Weapon:** a huge two-handed bearded greataxe (wide serrated cheek, a long hooked beard for the drag-pull, a back spike, a wrapped grip with a brass ring), blood on the steel. **Poses drawn:** guard (the axe held low across the body) and the overhead wind-up. **To make it a game sprite:** his coat, hat and mask as armour looks on the existing NPC body (`make_npc_looks.py`, like the Barbwire Coat), a held-axe weapon sprite, and moves with `melee_moves.py`.

### The Verger is a two-form fight (Yan, 2026-11-20: "he starts as a sprite swinging his scythe, similar to Visor and other melee fights, but at 50% he transforms into a beast, werewolf-like, still wearing his clothes, a boss with a sprite similar to the Gaunt, so he grows a lot and has new animations")
- **Weapon is a war scythe now** (not the axe above): `weapon_VergerScythe(_hd).png`, id `VergerScythe`. The axe in the concept sheet is out of date.
- **Form 1 (100-50 %):** the human duel on the Visor/Spool frame (armour look `Verger Coat`, held scythe, parry windows). Moves: overhead slash (parry window), wide sweep, hook-and-drag with the blade's inside curve, a spinning cut.
- **The change (at 50 %):** a short cutscene beat, no HP refill, no nerf (CLAUDE.md rule 6): he drops the scythe, the coat strains and tears at the seams, he hunches and grows (human 1 tile to a Gaunt-sized body, about 4 tiles tall), the hat falls off, the leather mask splits. **Not invulnerable (Yan, 2026-11-20): players can keep hitting him during the change, but he takes 90 % less damage until it ends** (a timed damage-reduction window on the Boss.cs phase beat, not a nerf to the beast form). The change itself is the telegraph; players get a breath to reposition.
- **Form 2 (50-0 %):** a werewolf-like beast *still wearing the torn coat, scarf and belt of vials* (the readable link to form 1), big, in the Gaunt's art style (dark, grey-skinned, tall, long limbs). New animations: idle, run on all fours, claw swipe (both arms), pounce/leap, howl (calls nothing: the world lives by itself, rule 7), enrage slam, hurt, death. Moves are the Boss.cs `Move`s with a telegraph each; no Husks during the fight (MISSION_CHOIR.md).
- **Art route:** form 2 is a Gaunt-quality sprite, so it needs SpriteCook/Pixel Lab (Yan has no credits right now) or a hand-built `pxart` sprite like the mutant concepts; the Gaunt's own clips came from Pixel Lab (`export_elder_clips.py`). Concept first, from his references.
- **Code (2026-11-20, built, never run in Unity):** `Boss.Verger` in `Boss.cs` (7000 HP; `Def.Change` = the generic half-health change: `BeginChange`, `DamageScale` 0.1 for 4 s, `Deflects` off while it lasts; `Move.Phase1Only/Phase2Only`), `VergerBoss.cs` (spawn/entrance/ending), `VergerScythe` (`Melee.Table`: reach 2.1, cuts and bleeds) and `VergerClaws` (the second form's weapon, 1.7 reach) in `WeaponDatabase` (in `WeaponDatabase.Unique`: no shop, no loot table), the scythe goes into his pack at the change, the coat at the execution (`HumanLootSystem.StashWeaponNow/StashArmorOnly`), Testing Ground > BOSS The Verger. Moves: form 1 Reap (overhead, parry), Sweep (2 hits, wide), Hook (red: he stops at the blade's tip, and a hit drags you up to 3.2 tiles toward him and drops you for half a second), Harvest (3 hits); form 2 Rake (3 fast hits), Pounce (red, lunge 6), Maul (red, heavy), Frenzy (5 hits). Harness: `VergerChecks`.
- **Beast sprite (built later the same day, hand-built concept poses, not final art):** `export_verger_beast.py` -> `Sprites/Mutants/anim/verger_beast.bytes` (stand, change, run, attack), drawn by `VergerBoss.BeastFrame` at `BeastScale` 0.5 (~3 tiles); a pose swap, no walk cycle, no hurt or death frames (the corpse is the man again).
- **Not built:** the cathedral job that sends him in at the end of every run (built later: CathedralJob.cs), co-op sync of the change (host-side only, like Spool), pt-BR lines for his name card and barks.

### The beast, retuned for the brawler body (2026-11-20; Yan picked a heavy, Bloodborne-priest-style werewolf over the gaunt one)
Form 2 is slow and heavy: `Beast Claws` 2.0 kg (Heavy style), 600 damage, reach 1.9, pace 1.1 (waits and openings a tenth longer). Moves: **Rake** (two hits, white glint, parry it), **Smash** (red, 1.9x, long wind-up and a 2.4 s opening), **Charge** (red, rushes up to 7 tiles, 1.5x), **Sweep** (one wide hit round him). Each plays a Pixel Lab clip timed so its hit frame lands on the strike (`VergerBoss.Clips`, `README_BEAST.md` in `ArtSource/mutants_anim/verger_beast/`); the change plays the roar. Not generated: idle, hurt, death (the corpse is the man again). Rake and sweep clips turn toward the camera: worth a re-roll (~2 generations each).

### The beast's combos (2026-11-21; Yan: "a few combos for the werewolf boss like the Gaunt has")
No new generation (the Pixel Lab trial is spent): the approved clips stitched where their silhouettes meet, as for the Gaunt's combo3/combo4 (`ArtSource/make_beast_combos.py` -> `verger_beast_<combo>.bytes`, `combos.json`). **Rake-Sweep** (two blows, 0.44 s apart, the second wide), **Rake-Smash** (a slash, then the Smash after the whole raise-the-arms telegraph, 1.16 s later: the second blow is red, 1.8x), **Rampage** (rake, sweep, smash: 0.44 s and 1.51 s apart, the last red and 1.9x; only below a quarter of his full health, so the last stretch of the beast). New Move fields: `Gaps` (the pause before each blow), `Mults` (each blow's damage), `RedHits` (which blows can't be parried), `BelowHp`. The beast's clips now play at the fight's pace (`Fight.ClipScale`, 1.1). The joins are not seamless (silhouette overlap 0.55-0.62; the Smash's raised-arm pose never matches the others closely).

### More moves for the beast (2026-11-21; Yan: "looks cool, but he needs more moves")
Four new moves from the existing clips (no generation): **Howl** (red; a cone across the room, 9 tiles: aim blown, deafened 2 s, knocked down 1 s, a dive goes under; the roar clip's first nine frames), **Quake** (red, 1.5x; the slam's shockwave as a RING round him, 4.2 tiles: everyone inside is hit and knocked down, a dive goes under, unparryable), **Trample** (charge, then the Smash after a 1.3 s telegraph; both blows red), **Howl-Rake** (the Howl, then a parryable rake 0.99 s later: the howl blows your aim, the rake punishes it). With the Rake, Smash, Charge, Sweep, Rake-Sweep, Rake-Smash and the Rampage (last quarter only) the beast has eleven moves. New Move fields: `Effects` (what a blow does instead of a swing: "howl", "quake"), `MaxDist` (how far away he may start it). Harness checks cover the Howl's reach, the dive, the Quake's ring.

### The Verger is the man with the scythe (2026-11-21)
Yan: the beast "looks very ai generated and his attacks don't read well, save it but take him out of the game for now and make the human version the entire boss, elaborate more on the human scythe-moveset".
- **The beast is parked**, not deleted: `Boss.VergerBeast` (the half-health change, the claws, the eleven beast moves), `VergerBoss.Clips`, the clip strips and the Pixel Lab sources are all kept; the harness still runs `VergerBeastChecks`. Nothing spawns it.
- **His own pose set** (`HeldPoseMath.VergerPoses`, `Style.Verger`, with a hit shape each in `Melee.Shapes`): 0 reap (overhead into the floor), 1 sweep (waist, all in the arc), 2 rising cut, 3 hook (thrown out, hauled back; the longest reach), 4 danse (a full turn, all round him), 5 ankle reap (low at your feet), 6 backhand (the sweep come back), 7 impale (point up, then down through you). He starts swings from two tiles out: he fights from the blade's tip.
- **Phase 1** (measured): Reap, Sweep (sweep + backhand), Rising Cut (the quick one), Hook (red, drags you in), Ankle Reap (red, dive over it), Harvest (3 blows).
- **Phase 2** at half health (no change: "He takes off the hat and sets it down on the flagstones. The mask stays on."; quicker): adds the Danse (two full turns), the Toll (red: the blade rung on the stone like the cathedral bell, deafens and blows your aim within 6 tiles, no damage, a dive goes under: the setup for the Hook), the Hook-Reap (hauled in, then a white reap you can parry), Reaper's Rush (red lunge, 6 tiles, the impale), the Widow's Harvest (five blows: sweep, back, the feet (red), up, down).
- **Last quarter**: the Last Rite (danse, backhand, ankle reap (red), impale (red)).
