# Boss creature designs (2026-11-20; Yan: "silent hill, resident evil, bloodborne style monsters")
Direction words (for the artist, never shown to players): wrongness over gore, one strong silhouette each, a human thing inside the monster (hands, a mouth, a uniform), a rhythm or a sound as the signature (the 11 s beat), and a single unreadable part (a face with no eyes, a mask). Muted palette plus ONE accent colour per boss. Original names only. Concept sprites are hand-built (`ArtSource/make_boss_concepts.py`), NOT in the game, and are concept quality, not final art.

## The Verger (concept, 2026-11-20; Yan: "give me the concept for that boss we talked about")
Gothic beast hunter, the cathedral's last verger (human boss on the Visor/Spool frame, `MISSION_CHOIR.md`). `ArtSource/make_verger_concept.py` -> `mutants_anim/boss_concepts/verger_concept_sheet.png` (+ `verger_guard_concept.png`, `verger_chop_concept.png`), hand-built with pxart, concept quality, NOT in the game. **Look:** a tall figure in a long dark coat with a high collar, ragged tails and a belt of pouches and blood vials; a wide-brimmed hat that drops his face into shadow, pale eyes, a stitched leather mask over the nose and mouth with a brass clasp; a red scarf (the one colour accent) hanging from the neck; gloves; boots. **Weapon:** a huge two-handed bearded greataxe (wide serrated cheek, a long hooked beard for the drag-pull, a back spike, a wrapped grip with a brass ring), blood on the steel. **Poses drawn:** guard (the axe held low across the body) and the overhead wind-up. **To make it a game sprite:** his coat, hat and mask as armour looks on the existing NPC body (`make_npc_looks.py`, like the Barbwire Coat), a held-axe weapon sprite, and moves with `melee_moves.py`.

### The Verger is a two-form fight (Yan, 2026-11-20: "he starts as a sprite swinging his scythe, similar to Visor and other melee fights, but at 50% he transforms into a beast, werewolf-like, still wearing his clothes, a boss with a sprite similar to the Gaunt, so he grows a lot and has new animations")
- **Weapon is a war scythe now** (not the axe above): `weapon_VergerScythe(_hd).png`, id `VergerScythe`. The axe in the concept sheet is out of date.
- **Form 1 (100-50 %):** the human duel on the Visor/Spool frame (armour look `Verger Coat`, held scythe, parry windows). Moves: overhead slash (parry window), wide sweep, hook-and-drag with the blade's inside curve, a spinning cut.
- **The change (at 50 %):** a short cutscene beat, no HP refill, no nerf (CLAUDE.md rule 6): he drops the scythe, the coat strains and tears at the seams, he hunches and grows (human 1 tile to a Gaunt-sized body, about 4 tiles tall), the hat falls off, the leather mask splits. **Not invulnerable (Yan, 2026-11-20): players can keep hitting him during the change, but he takes 90 % less damage until it ends** (a timed damage-reduction window on the Boss.cs phase beat, not a nerf to the beast form). The change itself is the telegraph; players get a breath to reposition.
- **Form 2 (50-0 %):** a werewolf-like beast *still wearing the torn coat, scarf and belt of vials* (the readable link to form 1), big, in the Gaunt's art style (dark, grey-skinned, tall, long limbs). New animations: idle, run on all fours, claw swipe (both arms), pounce/leap, howl (calls nothing: the world lives by itself, rule 7), enrage slam, hurt, death. Moves are the Boss.cs `Move`s with a telegraph each; no Husks during the fight (MISSION_CHOIR.md).
- **Art route:** form 2 is a Gaunt-quality sprite, so it needs SpriteCook/Pixel Lab (Yan has no credits right now) or a hand-built `pxart` sprite like the mutant concepts; the Gaunt's own clips came from Pixel Lab (`export_elder_clips.py`). Concept first, from his references.
- **Code (2026-11-20, built, never run in Unity):** `Boss.Verger` in `Boss.cs` (7000 HP; `Def.Change` = the generic half-health change: `BeginChange`, `DamageScale` 0.1 for 4 s, `Deflects` off while it lasts; `Move.Phase1Only/Phase2Only`), `VergerBoss.cs` (spawn/entrance/ending), `VergerScythe` (`Melee.Table`: reach 2.1, cuts and bleeds) and `VergerClaws` (the second form's weapon, 1.7 reach) in `WeaponDatabase` (in `WeaponDatabase.Unique`: no shop, no loot table), the scythe goes into his pack at the change, the coat at the execution (`HumanLootSystem.StashWeaponNow/StashArmorOnly`), Testing Ground > BOSS The Verger. Moves: form 1 Reap (overhead, parry), Sweep (2 hits, wide), Hook (red, lunge 4), Harvest (3 hits); form 2 Rake (3 fast hits), Pounce (red, lunge 6), Maul (red, heavy), Frenzy (5 hits). Harness: `VergerChecks`.
- **Not built:** the beast's own sprite (until it exists he fights on in the human body, claws only in the moves), the Hook's drag, the cathedral job that sends him in at the end of every run, co-op sync of the change (host-side only, like Spool), pt-BR lines for his name card and barks.
