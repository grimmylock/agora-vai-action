# Tiers 4-7 outline, bent toward the ending (draft, 2026-11-18, for Yan to approve)

Ending in one line: the mutants have woken up and are spreading the Zone over the world; "those" found it first and have been leashing and selling it; the player can't stop it. Tiers 1-2 stay as written. Tier 3 gets two small additions (below).
Rules that hold: rule 6 (never nerf, run/hide/go round instead), rule 7 (real squads, no spawns next to you), rule 9 (co-op), no mission needs a companion, the Surveyors are the scientists' faction.

## The thread: the player learns it in small steps

Each tier gives one clue that the mutants are not just being pushed around, and one that "those" are holding a leash.

| Tier | The clue about the mutants | The clue about "those" |
|-|-|-|
| 1-2 (as written) | something pushes deep-Zone mutants south; husks | the Grey Robes test a mind device |
| 3 | the brain in the vat (already built) reacts to the Sub-Station's signal tests, not to the people | Rask (the traitor) is "those"; the Veteran was one |
| 4 | at the Barrier the mutants stop at the fanatics' line and wait, as if listening to the Preacher's psi-core | the Preacher's core was handed to the Obelisk by "those", not made by them |
| 5 | when the array dies, every mutant on the map goes quiet, then turns the same way (toward the centre): the signal wasn't controlling them, they were answering it | the Array Commander is "those"; his song is the leash, he says "it" will not stay on it |
| 6 | the hospital's deep-lab portal opens onto a place that is not a lab: tissue, wiring, one mind | the Garrison commander took orders from "those" and never knew; a captured insider: "we were never building a weapon, we were building a leash" |
| 7 | the bottom: the Mind (working name), conscious, patient, not hostile in the way a beast is | the transmitters in crates are the leash, ready to ship to the world |

## Tier 3 - The Safe Haven (additions only)
- Dr. Osei's measurements (the energy spikes from deeper underground) are the Mind's heartbeat; she calls it interference.
- Rask's reveal also shows his orders: keep the counter-signal project slow. The countermeasure was designed to fail.
- Flags: Rask exposed or not by the player's own work, Hale cleared or not, the Veteran's medication found or not.

## Tier 4 - The Barrier
- Set piece: the siege (RPG man, LMG nest, grenade swarms) and the Preacher (purple waves, dive through them). Kill him without breaking the core.
- New: mutants gather behind the fanatics and do not attack them. When the core breaks (or is taken), they scatter, then regroup farther in.
- Politics: the Sentinels and Liberators truce, or not (flag). Your choice of who holds the line decides who holds a base later.
- Hand-over / destroy the core: stays as a choice. (Hand it to the Garrison and "those" get it.)

## Tier 5 - The Array
- Serpentine road of checkpoints, the Array Commander (shield, adds, Tolik's tackle if present, or a scripted gap if not), the 3-minute broadcast.
- New: the quiet after. The whole map's mutants face one direction for a minute. That is the first moment the player sees it is not a weapon.
- Husks released from the array stay "dead inside" (as in the logs).

## Tier 6 - The Siege Ward
- The hospital: Garrison base, and where "those" fall back. Ambush on the bridge, the prisoner interrogated.
- The science team opens a portal to the deep lab; you shoot the computer (the player takes the shot) or push through before it closes (two ways, flag).
- The reveal: the captured insider says leash, not weapon. Garrison leaders who survive learn they were used.
- Optional: the Mind's "voice" first speaks here, through a husk: one line, no threat.

## Tier 7 - The Descent
- The guide, the freight elevator (Lev: "this is where I walked out"), the wire across the airlock, fog, gas, the race with the fanatics.
- The bottom: the transmitters ready to ship, "those" with their calm voice, and the Mind.
- **How you leave** (Yan unsure; three ways to think about it):
  1. **Cut the leash** (the counter-signal goes out). The Mind is free and spreads faster. The tragic, honest version of "the countermeasure was too late". Hope: the husks wake up themselves (their minds were in there).
  2. **Take the leash** ("those" offer it; you or a faction hold the transmitters). The spread is slower, but "those" are in charge of what comes. A dark version. Hope: none, which is the point.
  3. **Listen**: you walk out with its message (a map of where it will go, a list of who it will spare). A small, strange hope: it can be talked to. Needs the most writing.
- My suggestion: all three lead to the same changed world (the player can't stop it), but each decides the aftermath: which factions still hold bases (from the Tier 4 and 6 flags), whether Bonfire Village stays safe, what the husks do, and what "those" do next. One hope thread that all endings keep: the husk cure data, held by Pavel/Osei.

## After the ending (the changed world)
- Every zone Tier 5 or above, mutants stronger and bolder (attack anything that moves), factions hold bases, a few elite squads roam for supplies.
- Bases call you on the PDA to defend (the Tier 1 horde mission, reused), and only then the hordes come. Rewards scale up.
- New paths and secrets in labs and dungeons (a deeper level behind each existing lock).

2. Then Tier 2 (already agreed), then Tier 3's additions.
3. The ending decision can wait until Tier 6; only the flags have to exist before it.
