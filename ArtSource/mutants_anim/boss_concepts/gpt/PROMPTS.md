# ChatGPT prompts for the Ferryman and the Orderly (2026-11-21)
Attach `style_reference_sheet.png` (our Pallid Prowler, Gaunt, Blight Brute, Mother Hollow and a 1-tile player, all at the same scale) to each prompt. The rules behind them: `HORROR_DESIGN_REFS.md`.
Ask for 2-3 versions each. Save the ones you like into this folder (`ferryman_*.png`, `orderly_*.png`); I take them to game size, clean the alpha and animate them with Pixel Lab (the Mother Hollow pipeline).

## The Ferryman
```
Pixel art game sprite, ONE creature, side view facing left, on a plain transparent (or flat grey) background.
Match the attached reference sheet exactly in style: the same pixel size, the same simple shading with few colours, the same dark 1-pixel outline, the same muted palette. It must look like it belongs next to those creatures, not more detailed than them.
Size: about 3 times the height of the small player on the right of the sheet, a long low crawling body.

The creature: "The Ferryman", a horror boss in the style of Silent Hill and Dead Space. A drowned corpse that became an anglerfish. Its head is a bloated grey human torso split open down the middle into a huge vertical mouth, the broken ribs are its teeth. No eyes. It crawls on four long thin human arms bent backwards at the elbow, used as legs, with long fingers. A bare spine grows out of its back and curves forward over the mouth like an angler's lure, and from it hangs an old ship's lantern glowing sickly green, with a pale drowned human face pressed against the glass from inside. Rotten rope and torn fishing net have grown through its grey-green waterlogged skin. Water drips off it.
It should look miserable and wrong, made of people, not like an animal or a fish. One accent colour: the lantern's green.
```

## The Orderly
```
Pixel art game sprite, ONE creature, side view facing left, on a plain transparent (or flat grey) background.
Match the attached reference sheet exactly in style: the same pixel size, the same simple shading with few colours, the same dark 1-pixel outline, the same muted palette. It must look like it belongs next to those creatures, not more detailed than them.
Size: about 3.5 times the height of the small player on the right of the sheet, tall and hunched.

The creature: "The Orderly", a horror boss in the style of Silent Hill and Dead Space. An insect made out of a hospital patient. A pale grey human torso stretched long into an insect thorax, bent forward. Its two arms have grown into long jagged bone-saw blades held up like a praying mantis. A black hospital body bag is fused over its back like beetle wing cases, half unzipped. IV tubes run into its body from a hanging bag of dark blood on a bent drip pole growing out of its back. A stretched human face is on the underside of its belly, mouth open. It stands on six thin legs made of human forearms and fingers. Scraps of a stained surgical gown hang off it.
It should look miserable and wrong, made of people, not like a real insect. One accent colour: arterial red (the blood bag, the wounds).
```
