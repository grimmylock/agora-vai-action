# Pixel Lab animations for the Rot Crawler and Mind Weaver (2026-11-20)
Yan liked those two concepts (the Void Weaver "we'll revisit"). Helper: `ArtSource/pixellab_anim.py` (sprite centred on a canvas bottom, `animate-pixminimax`, `first_frame = last_frame` for loops). Trial credits: 19.05 of 40 generations before these two.
- `rot_walk/` (crawl loop): 9 frames, canvas 160x100, seed 11, job 26ebcb7f. Prompt: crawls forward in place on all fours in a steady loop: the long arms reach and plant, the haunches heave forward, the spine arches and the fungus shelves bob, the head sways low with the jaw swinging. First/last overlap 0.999, body drift ~12 px. `rot_walk_loop.gif`, `rot_walk_sheet.png`.
- `mind_hover/` (float loop): 9 frames, canvas 150x180, seed 21, job a129b560. Prompt: hovers in place in a slow loop: the body sways gently, the long fingers weave and the strung threads between them swing, the loose nerves below drift and sway, the head tilts slightly. First/last overlap 0.985, drift ~1 px. Subtle. `mind_hover_loop.gif`, `mind_hover_sheet.png`.
Not wired into the game; waiting on Yan's look. Still to do if approved: attacks (Rot Crawler bite/pounce; Mind Weaver psi cast), death, and the game sprite (`mutant_hd_*.png`).
