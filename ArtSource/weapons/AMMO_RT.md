# Ammunition by caliber (2026-11-17)

Yan: separate the weapons by caliber, have the actual ammo in the game, and balance with it too. Every gun's `AmmoType` is its caliber; rounds are kept per caliber and load (`Ammo.cs`, pool key `<caliber>|<kind>`), the loads are in `GameData/AmmoDatabase.cs` (edit a row to rebalance). Generated by `RTBAL=4 bash Tools/compile_check/runtest.sh ../../Assets/Scripts`.

- **Standard** is the baseline every gun was tuned against (`ArtSource/weapons/WEAPON_TUNING_RT.md`): damage x1.0 and armor x1.0, except where a caliber's own round already pierces (9x39 subsonic, 5.7x28, 4.6x30, 9x21, 12.7x55, .50 BMG). The other loads are the balance lever: `damage` multiplies the round's damage, `armor x` multiplies the target's Phys-DR (0.5 = pierces half of it), `hollow` = hollow-point rule (+25% on the unarmored, -50% on armor of 20%+), `map tier` = the first map tier whose traders, stock and loot carry the load.
- The shop sells, for each caliber you carry a gun for (hands, second slot, off hand, pack), the loads the map's tier has reached; boxes cost round price x rounds, before remoteness, reputation and Haggler. Loot and drops give the caliber of the gun in your hands (the load too if the map has reached it). Old saves: pools of the old form (`rifle3|HollowPoint`, bare kinds) are converted to the caliber of the gun in your hands when it is of that class, else to 9x19mm / 5.45x39mm / 7.62x54mmR / 12g / rail cells.
