Drawn in Python/PIL by `make_mutants_humanoid.py`; sprites in `ArtSource/mutants_anim/humanoid/` (NOT in the game yet: the old 16 px atlas cells are still used). Names are the existing database names; the concepts replace what the old cells showed (the atlas cell for Alpha Prowler already was a humanoid; the others were a rat, a jellyfish and a hooded blob).

| Mutant (tier) | Concept | Why it reads | Tells to animate later |

| **Alpha Prowler** (T3, ambush, cloak, bleeds you) | A lean, hunched, long-armed hunter: narrow body, ribs and spine showing, knuckles that reach the ground, one red eye on a narrow muzzle of teeth. The cloaked version is the same body pale grey. | Predator silhouette, clearly bigger and leaner than a man; the arms say "reach", the red eye is the only warm colour | the lunge (arms swing forward, claws spread), the cloak shimmer |

**Redo (2026-11-19, same day; Yan: "redo them like you did the gaunt, he looked a lot better").** The first three drawings above were too small and cartoonish; replaced by `ArtSource/make_concepts_v2.py` (same method and scale as `make_gaunt.py`: anatomy first, muted colours, hand-placed details). `make_concepts_remaining.py` is the superseded first try. Files are the same names in `ArtSource/mutants_anim/concepts_new/`. Sizes: Rot Crawler 115x65, Mind Weaver 97x153, Void Weaver 128x167. What changed: **Rot Crawler** now a crawling human body (frog-folded hind legs, shoulder blades, ribs through a torn flank, ochre bracket fungus with gills up the spine, a dislocated jaw, one spore-lit eye); **Mind Weaver** an emaciated stooped figure (collarbones, ribs, hollow belly, a long hanging spine with loose nerves, a cracked-open skull round a bare brain, sewn eyes, a violet cat's cradle of thread between its fingers); **Void Weaver** a stooped, forward-leaning robe with a heron-necked hood, a small mask with one slit set deep inside, a torn train, one arm reaching with six long fingers, cracks of light and a broken halo. Still rough: the Void Weaver's cloth is flat purple; the Mind Weaver's lower nerve spray reads as a net; the cloaked variants are not drawn.
